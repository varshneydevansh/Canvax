    Canvax guide

    How This Works

    Use Frame view to sketch and annotate a screen. Use Flow view to arrange frames and define how the experience moves between them.

    Workbench

    • Use Surface to choose desktop, mobile, tablet, poster, square, or a large free canvas.
    • New frame creates another screen or state. New section creates a connected continuation frame.
    • Make real turns the current sketch into a local generated surface.
    • Open scratchpad switches into designer focus so the canvas becomes the main surface. Use Show brief to bring the controls back.
    • The floating designer rail keeps Select, Pen, Rect, Arrow, Erase, Undo, Redo, Talk, Make, and Apply available even when the tray is hidden.
    • Draw over the generated surface to save visual correction marks that Codex can read with the frame.
    • Apply to Codex saves one clear sketch + voice checkpoint for this chat.

    Canvas basics

    • Pen and Marker are freehand tools.
    • Line, Rect, Oval, and Arrow create structured shapes.
    • Freeze frame saves now. Autosnap updates the live handoff after two seconds idle.

    Selection and grouping

    • Use Select to click an element, drag to move it, or drag corner handles to resize it.
    • Hold Shift and click to build a multi-selection.
    • Group makes that relationship persistent so clicking one grouped item selects the full set.
    • In Map, Group wraps selected Map objects in a region. Ungroup removes the region without deleting the objects inside. Select contents points back to the contained Map objects, and Fit group resizes the region around its contents.
    • Duplicate sel creates an offset copy. Send back and Bring front change layer order.

    Labels

    • Click empty canvas to place a free note.
    • Click over a shape to attach the label to that element so it follows move and resize.
    • Type inline on the canvas. Enter places it, Shift+Enter adds a new line, and Esc cancels.

    Voice notes

    • Use Current frame to tie spoken notes to the active screen, or Whole board for broader direction.
    • Start dictation uses browser speech recognition when supported.
    • If browser voice is unavailable, paste macOS dictation or type into Manual voice note and press Add note.

    Flow and captures

    • Flow view arranges frames and connects them with arrows and transition labels.
    • Reference underlay places a screenshot below your sketch.
    • Captures are saved frame snapshots. Remove one capture at a time or clear them all from the Captures section.
